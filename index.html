Varsity.com Evolution

Why evolve Varsity.com now?

  • Growth is also defense: lower barriers to entry make it easier for competitors to move quickly into the digital spaces where customers discover, evaluate, and act. Varsity must lead in AI-powered discovery, personalized journeys, connected experiences, and fast content delivery.
  • The current site is reaching the end of its technical life and is increasingly difficult to maintain, update, and scale.
  • Customers need a simpler way to find the right Varsity offering, event, resource, or next step across a growing portfolio of brands and audiences.
  • Our business has outgrown the site’s original structure, requiring a more flexible foundation for new offerings, content types, audiences, and customer needs.
  • A modern platform can improve content management, mobile experience, performance, accessibility, search visibility, AI-driven discovery, and connection across the broader Varsity ecosystem.
