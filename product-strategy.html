Product Strategy

Varsity.com Evolution

Varsity needs a digital front door for the next decade, staged to capitalize on changes in technology and serve the mobile-first customer.

Evolve Varsity.com into a strategic growth engine for the business.

North Star

Drive growth across the Varsity ecosystem by evolving Varsity.com into a high-performing discovery, conversion, and routing platform for athletes, students, parents, families, fans, coaches, advisers, and gym and studio owners.

Overview

Executive Summary

Varsity.com is evolving into a growth platform for the Varsity ecosystem. Its purpose is to increase participation, fan engagement, revenue-generating handoffs, lead generation, audience reach, brand authority, and repeat engagement across Events, Cheer, Dance, Yearbook, Performing Arts, Fashion and Uniforms, Watch Online, Shop, My Team Shop, myVarsity, and support.

Product Strategy

Goals

1

Grow event participation and event fan engagement

Increase discovery, registration, attendance, watching, schedule engagement, results engagement, ticket purchases, event merchandise activity, and repeat visits around camps, competitions, championships, workshops, and special events.

What we heard

“Event content goes stale every year, there's no single owner for site-wide content strategy, and marketing needs a CMS they can update without developer support — even as that content is one of the site's most valuable, most underused assets. There's also no clear system for handling two very different jobs content has to do at once: keeping operationally critical information (dates, rules, logistics) accurate, while also building the discovery content that brings new audiences in. Right now, one tends to crowd out the other.”

2

Grow conversion across the Varsity ecosystem

Move more visitors into high-value next steps, including myVarsity registration and payments, ticketing, Varsity TV, Shop, My Team Shop, catalog requests, apparel rep contact, key Yearbook touchpoints, CLI Studios, and support. Visitors are confident about what they can do next.

What we heard

“Contact and lead forms, and their routing, are a major pain point needing a fresh look. The goal is less information required before reaching the right team — landing pages need the same attention.”

What we heard

“The current WordPress site wasn't built for e-commerce and is creating operational strain. Any new solution needs to be simple to integrate with Shopify — but not a heavy enterprise platform for a modest catalog.”

3

Grow audience reach and organic demand capture

Build stronger SEO and AI-search visibility through authoritative pages for events, camps, dance, cheer, yearbook, performing arts, fashion, uniforms, rules, schedules, results, and support topics.

What we heard

“A large volume of content built up over years supports traditional SEO but isn't structured for AI-driven discovery — real value sitting idle, and one of the most significant current gaps.”

4

Increase full Varsity business-line visibility

Give Cheer, Dance, Yearbook, Performing Arts, Fashion and Uniforms, Watch Online, and Shop stronger entry points while connecting related Varsity offerings so visitors can easily move from one need to the next.

What we heard

“The site serves many audiences — athletes, parents, coaches, spectators — but hasn't consistently delivered the best experience for each. Discovery content, like finding a camp or gym, is often missing because it isn't top of mind for teams creating product-specific content.”

6

Grow relevance through personalization

Adapt content, offers, and next-best actions based on visitor behavior, interests, seasonality, event engagement, login status, and known relationship with Varsity.

7

Modernize the platform for scalable growth

Create stronger content models, event data structure, analytics, governance, redirects, page templates, accessibility, performance, and publishing workflows.

What we heard

“Most needed data should be available through GA4, but it isn't well configured — lead and SEO tracking both remain unresolved.”

Strategies

1

Make Events a growth engine

Right now, the vast majority of traffic to varsity.com is for event information. Continue to serve this audience by creating one powerful, user-friendly, connected experience for camps, competitions, schedules, results, registration, tickets, event gear, Varsity TV, and related support. Connect related Varsity offerings to each event.

Product Strategy

Tactics To Achieve the Varsity.com Evolution Goals

Create canonical event hub pages

Build major event pages as complete source-of-truth hubs with dates, location, registration, schedules, results, tickets, livestreams, merchandise, travel, rules, FAQs, support, and related Varsity offerings.

Use modular content blocks

Create reusable content modules for events, camps, merch, Varsity TV, Shop, My Team Shop, support, tryouts, seasonal moments, and business-line promotions so pages can adapt without custom rebuilding every time.

Design audience-based pathways

Create clear paths for athletes, students, parents, families, fans, coaches, advisers, gym owners, and studio owners, with relevant next steps for each group.

Make every handoff measurable

Track clicks and conversions to myVarsity, registration, payments, ticketing, Varsity TV, Shop, My Team Shop, catalog requests, rep contact, CLI Studios, Yearbook, Performing Arts, and support.

Build next-best-action modules

Use page context and visitor behavior to surface smart actions like register, buy tickets, watch, view results, shop event merchandise, contact a rep, request a catalog, or prep for tryouts. Connect related offerings across the ecosystem, such as event details to tickets, livestreams, results, merchandise, support, and account actions; school uniforms to camps and competitions; and dance content to CLI Studios, camps, and events.

Implement personalization rules

Use anonymous behavior, cookies, Varsity.com login, myVarsity login, and SSO signals to tailor content based on known interests, event engagement, audience type, seasonality, and prior actions.

Create structured, AI-friendly content

Use clear headings, FAQs, tables, short paragraphs, direct answers, schema markup, canonical pages, fresh dates, and strong entity clarity for brands, events, locations, products, and Varsity relationships.

Modernize search and discovery

Improve search across competitions, camps, schedules, results, livestreams, products, support, and brands with filters, autocomplete, relevance tuning, and mobile-first usability.

Improve mobile-first performance

Prioritize fast load times, simple navigation, accessible HTML, responsive layouts, clear CTAs, and low-friction journeys for users arriving from search, social, email, and event-day traffic.

Create governance and publishing workflows

Define ownership, approval flows, update cycles, content models, metadata standards, redirects, archive rules, and QA requirements.

Build dashboards around the North Star

Create launch dashboards that measure event discovery, registration starts, ticket clicks, Varsity TV handoffs, merchandise activity, Shop/My Team Shop referrals, support deflection, search engagement, personalization performance, and repeat visits.
