Product Strategy

Varsity.com Evolution

Varsity needs a digital front door for the next decade, staged to capitalize on changes in technology and serve the mobile-first customer.

Evolve Varsity.com into a strategic growth engine for the business.

North Star

Drive growth across the Varsity ecosystem by evolving Varsity.com into a high-performing discovery, conversion, and routing platform for athletes, students, parents, families, fans, coaches, advisers, and gym and studio owners.

Overview

Executive Summary

Varsity.com is evolving into a growth platform for the Varsity ecosystem. Its purpose is to increase participation, fan engagement, revenue-generating handoffs, lead generation, audience reach, brand authority, and repeat engagement across Events, Cheer, Dance, Yearbook, Performing Arts, Fashion and Uniforms, Watch Online, Shop, My Team Shop, myVarsity, and support.

Product Strategy

Goals

1

Grow event participation and event fan engagement

Increase discovery, registration, attendance, watching, schedule engagement, results engagement, ticket purchases, event merchandise activity, and repeat visits around camps, competitions, championships, workshops, and special events.

What we heard

“Event content goes stale every year, there's no single owner for site-wide content strategy, and marketing needs a CMS they can update without developer support — even as that content is one of the site's most valuable, most underused assets. There's also no clear system for handling two very different jobs content has to do at once: keeping operationally critical information (dates, rules, logistics) accurate, while also building the discovery content that brings new audiences in. Right now, one tends to crowd out the other.”

2

Grow conversion across the Varsity ecosystem

Move more visitors into high-value next steps, including myVarsity registration and payments, ticketing, Varsity TV, Shop, My Team Shop, catalog requests, apparel rep contact, key Yearbook touchpoints, CLI Studios, and support. Visitors are confident about what they can do next.

What we heard

“Contact and lead forms, and their routing, are a major pain point needing a fresh look. The goal is less information required before reaching the right team — landing pages need the same attention.”

What we heard

“The current WordPress site wasn't built for e-commerce and is creating operational strain. Any new solution needs to be simple to integrate with Shopify — but not a heavy enterprise platform for a modest catalog.”

3

Grow audience reach and organic demand capture

Build stronger SEO and AI-search visibility through authoritative pages for events, camps, dance, cheer, yearbook, performing arts, fashion, uniforms, rules, schedules, results, and support topics.

What we heard

“A large volume of content built up over years supports traditional SEO but isn't structured for AI-driven discovery — real value sitting idle, and one of the most significant current gaps.”

4

Increase full Varsity business-line visibility

Give Cheer, Dance, Yearbook, Performing Arts, Fashion and Uniforms, Watch Online, and Shop stronger entry points while connecting related Varsity offerings so visitors can easily move from one need to the next.

What we heard

“The site serves many audiences — athletes, parents, coaches, spectators — but hasn't consistently delivered the best experience for each. Discovery content, like finding a camp or gym, is often missing because it isn't top of mind for teams creating product-specific content.”

6

Grow relevance through personalization

Adapt content, offers, and next-best actions based on visitor behavior, interests, seasonality, event engagement, login status, and known relationship with Varsity.

7

Modernize the platform for scalable growth

Create stronger content models, event data structure, analytics, governance, redirects, page templates, accessibility, performance, and publishing workflows.

What we heard

“Most needed data should be available through GA4, but it isn't well configured — lead and SEO tracking both remain unresolved.”

Strategies

1

Make Events a growth engine

Right now, the vast majority of traffic to varsity.com is for event information. Continue to serve this audience by creating one powerful, user-friendly, connected experience for camps, competitions, schedules, results, registration, tickets, event gear, Varsity TV, and related support. Connect related Varsity offerings to each event.

Design around audience intent

Protect the growth opportunity through technical discipline

Product Strategy

Tactics To Achieve the Varsity.com Evolution Goals

Category

Site Experience & Accessibility

Improve mobile-first performance

Prioritize fast load times, simple navigation, accessible HTML, responsive layouts, clear CTAs, and low-friction journeys for users arriving from search, social, email, and event-day traffic. Do this by setting performance budgets, optimizing images and scripts, using responsive components, and testing priority journeys on real mobile devices.

Make accessibility and inclusive publishing a release requirement

Require accessible HTML, predictive alt text, contrast and heading checks, descriptive links, captions, transcripts, keyboard navigation, and ADA-focused QA before publishing. Build these checks into design, content authoring, and release QA workflows.

Category

Measurement & Optimization

Build dashboards around the North Star

Create launch dashboards that measure event discovery, registration starts, ticket clicks, Varsity TV handoffs, merchandise activity, Shop/My Team Shop referrals, support deflection, search engagement, personalization performance, and repeat visits. Define shared KPIs, connect the necessary analytics sources, and review results on a regular operating cadence.

Make every handoff measurable

Track clicks and conversions to myVarsity, registration, payments, ticketing, Varsity TV, Shop, My Team Shop, catalog requests, rep contact, CLI Studios, Yearbook, Performing Arts, and support. Apply consistent tracking and conversion definitions to every outbound link, form, and cross-platform journey.

Category

Conversion & Lead Experience

Modernize conversion experiences

Improve native lead forms, routing, landing pages, catalog experiences, and commerce handoffs so customers can reach the right next step with less friction. Redesign high-value pages and forms around fewer required fields, smarter routing, and progressive disclosure—only showing the next question when it is relevant.

Category

Content Foundation

Use modular content blocks

Create reusable content modules for events, camps, merch, Varsity TV, Shop, My Team Shop, support, tryouts, seasonal moments, and business-line promotions so pages can adapt without custom rebuilding every time. Create and govern a library of configurable components in the CMS for these common content and conversion patterns.

Utilize a headless CMS (Content Management System)

Adopt a headless CMS to give content teams a flexible, governed way to create, manage, and publish structured content across Varsity.com and future digital channels. Select and implement a CMS with reusable content blocks, event and business-line models, metadata standards, approval workflows, role-based permissions, and API-based delivery so teams can update high-value information quickly without developer support.

Create structured, AI-friendly content

Use clear headings, FAQs, tables, short paragraphs, direct answers, schema markup, canonical pages, fresh dates, and strong entity clarity for brands, events, locations, products, and Varsity relationships. Define content templates and metadata standards that require reusable facts about each item, such as event dates, venue, audience, registration link, and associated Varsity business line.

Create governance and publishing workflows

Assign content owners, define how quickly the website should reflect changes made to event data such as schedules, establish approval paths and lifecycle rules for when content unpublishes or republishes, and schedule automated quality reviews for broken links, missing metadata, accessibility issues, and outdated event information.

Build content intelligence and localization workflows

Set up a shared system for images, videos, documents, and translated content so teams can tag assets, confirm usage rights, find approved versions, reuse assets, translate content, and review it before publishing.

Category

Findability

Create canonical event hub pages

A canonical event hub page is the single authoritative destination for an event, bringing its essential information, actions, and related Varsity experiences together in one consistently updated page. Build reusable event-page templates powered by a shared event data model and governed content modules, including dates, location, registration, schedules, results, tickets, livestreams, merchandise, travel, rules, FAQs, support, and related Varsity offerings.

Power event pages with connected live data

Use centralized event data to keep camps, competitions, Spirit Days, special events, locations, registration, schedules, results, tickets, and livestream links current across customer touchpoints. Connect event, registration, schedule, results, ticketing, and streaming systems through APIs or managed data feeds.

Design audience-based pathways

Use audience research and site behavior to create clear paths for athletes, students, parents, families, fans, coaches, advisers, gym owners, and studio owners, with relevant next steps for each group. For example, a cheer competition visitor could quickly find schedules, tickets, results, livestreams, and event merchandise; a school coach exploring Fashion & Uniforms could find catalog integration, My Team Shop, and a fashion representative.

Modernize search and discovery

Implement a search platform with centralized indexing, filters, autocomplete, synonyms, relevance tuning, and search analytics across competitions, camps, schedules, results, livestreams, products, support, and brands.

Implement a robust and accurate global search

Provide a fast, intuitive site-wide search experience that returns relevant results across events, camps, schedules, results, livestreams, products, support, and Varsity brands. Establish search governance, content-indexing standards, result-quality testing, autocomplete, filters, relevance tuning, and ongoing query-performance optimization.

Category

Personalization

Implement personalization rules

Show more relevant content based on what a visitor is doing on the site, while honoring their privacy choices. For example, someone repeatedly viewing Dance events could see upcoming Dance camps, related event information, and appropriate merchandise instead of generic Varsity content.

Build a connected data foundation optimized for website personalization

Create a unified customer profile from consented data across event, registration, CRM, marketing, commerce, streaming, and account systems. Build an identity-resolution and integration layer that makes approved data available for more relevant content, recommendations, offers, and next-best actions across Varsity.com.

Category

Retail Commerce

Enable retail commerce

Improve product discovery, direct shopping, consumer merchandise, and checkout handoffs so fans and families can move confidently from browsing to purchase. Connect product data, shopping experiences, merchandising rules, and checkout handoffs through a streamlined commerce integration.

Category

B2B Commerce

Support B2B commerce journeys

Connect catalog integrations, My Team Shop, TeamShop, and fashion-representative discovery so schools, programs, and other business customers can reach the right purchasing path and support. Use clear role- and organization-based paths that direct each customer to the appropriate purchasing option or representative.
