# Varsity.com Experience Notes

## The Big Idea

Varsity.com should help every visitor move with confidence, whether they are planning a season, finding a competition, registering for camp, watching Varsity TV, shopping for gear, or getting help.

## What Visitors Can Do

- Find competitions, camps, schedules, rules, results, and event details.
- Follow Varsity TV live coverage, upcoming events, and replays.
- Explore uniforms, catalogs, team looks, sizing, and rep support.
- Shop shoes, apparel, accessories, and event merchandise.
- Keep registrations, payments, saved teams, and next steps close at hand.
- Get clear answers for common event, account, payment, and support questions.

## How It Feels

- Clear, direct, and upbeat.
- Easy to scan.
- Helpful without overexplaining.
- Official without feeling stiff.
- Built around coaches, parents, athletes, fans, and teams.

## Brand Direction

- Uses Varsity's published wordmark treatment and Varsity Spirit brand colors.
- Keeps the visual system bold, clean, spirited, and photo-forward.
- Avoids invented logos or off-brand decorative marks.
