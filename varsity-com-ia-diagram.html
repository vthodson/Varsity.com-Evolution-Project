Cheer and Dance Program Structure

Cheer and Dance remain separate top-level doors. School and All Star pages use compact brand-logo modules at the bottom, and each page only shows brands for its own program.

Events Flow

Events is a shared engine for camps, competitions, special events, schedules, results, commerce, Watch Online, and registration/status/payment handoffs.

Discover

Find an event

  • Search by event name, brand, city, state, activity, or type
  • Filter by activity, audience, competition/camp, and date
  • Results rank by relevance first, then date
->
Evaluate

Event results

  • Cards show event type, activity, audience, date, location
  • Competitions can show tickets and event gear
  • Camp events do not show tickets
  • No news module appears on event result pages
->
Act

Event detail

  • When and where, planning, support, rules/forms
  • Register / manage / check status / make payment through myVarsity
  • Ticketing, event gear, Watch Online, schedule, results
  • Event-specific news only
School camp rule

Camps apply to School Cheer and School Dance. Camps do not apply to All Star Cheer or All Star Dance.

Commerce rule

Competition detail pages can route to tickets and event gear. Camp pages do not offer ticket purchase.

News rule

Event detail pages only include news about that event. School camp pages may also include general camp prep guidance.

Knowledge, News, and Content Placement

The Spirit Industry Knowledge Hub supports category authority, AI-powered results, and evergreen education. News supports timely updates without becoming a top-level navigation item.

Evergreen authority

Spirit Industry Knowledge Hub

  • Guides for beginner, parent, athlete, coach, adviser, director, and industry questions
  • Participation pathways, school tryouts, band and marching arts, yearbook, camps, competitions, rules, rankings, training, and career paths
  • Connected from footer, search, Cheer, Dance, Events, Performing Arts, Yearbook, audience pages, and event detail pages
Program discovery

Cheer Gym and Dance Studio Directory

  • Searchable and filterable directory for cheer gyms and dance studios
  • Filters by type, state, and program: School, All Star, Recreational, Training
  • Prototype uses sample listings; production needs a verified directory data source and listing governance
Discoverability

News hub

  • Accessible from footer, search, and contextual modules
  • Filters by Cheer, Dance, Events, Yearbook, Performing Arts, Fashion and Uniforms, Watch Online
  • Structured by activity, audience, brand, event type, and related event when available
Contextual modules

Page-level news

  • Cheer pages show Cheer news
  • Dance pages show Dance news
  • Audience pages keep School and All Star news separate
  • Fashion and Uniforms shows apparel/uniform content
Event detail only

Event news rules

  • No news module on Find an Event results
  • Event detail pages include news about that event only
  • School camp pages can include general camp prep guidance

Handoff Layer

Varsity.com helps users choose the right path, then hands them to the platform where the task is completed.

Account and events

myVarsity

Most event registration, management, status checks, rosters, and payments.

Commerce

Ticketing and Event Gear

Competition tickets and event merchandise when available.

Watch

Varsity TV

Live coverage, upcoming coverage, replays, saved teams, and The Varsity Cheer League standings.

Shopping

Shop and My Team Shop

Shop is individual commerce. My Team Shop is private school/team commerce.

Dance training

CLI Studios

Dance classes, teacher training, conservatory programs, auditions, and intensives.

Yearbook

Order Center and eShare

Yearbook purchases, ads, dedications, and photo submission.

Sales

Rep routing

Separate uniform/catalog rep paths for Cheer, Dance, Band Wear, Color Guard Wear, and Performing Arts needs.

Support

Help Center

Event, account, order, ticket, event gear, uniform, team shop, and yearbook help.
