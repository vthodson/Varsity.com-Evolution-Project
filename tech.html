Capability Recommendation Why
Frontend Framework Next.js / React
  • Varsity.com needs a modern frontend architecture because the current ecosystem spans content, registration, media, commerce, and operational workflows that must feel unified across devices.
  • A modern frontend layer is required to support mobile-first experiences, personalization, scalability, performance, and reusable UI components across brands.
  • Next.js and React are recommended because they are industry-leading frameworks for composable enterprise platforms, support server-side rendering and SEO, integrate well with headless CMS platforms, and provide a strong ecosystem for long-term scalability. ERMP uses this.
CMS Contentful / Sanity / Contentstack / AWS CMS?
  • Varsity.com needs a headless CMS because content is currently distributed across brands, events, media, commerce, and digital marketing experiences that require faster publishing and better governance.
  • A headless CMS enables structured, reusable content that can power web, mobile, streaming, email, and future digital channels from a single source.
  • Contentful, Sanity, and Contentstack are recommended because they are enterprise-grade, API-first platforms that support modular content modeling, localization, governance workflows, and composable architectures.
Commerce Shopify Plus / commercetools
  • Varsity.com needs a modern commerce platform because apparel, merchandise, and event-related products are core monetization channels that must integrate seamlessly with media, events, and user identity.
  • Current commerce experiences are fragmented and disconnected from the broader ecosystem.
  • Shopify Plus is recommended for operational simplicity and rapid modernization, while commercetools is recommended if Varsity pursues a more enterprise-grade composable commerce architecture with deeper customization and multi-system orchestration.
Search Algolia / Elastic
  • Varsity.com needs modern search because users currently navigate a large ecosystem of camps, competitions, livestreams, products, brands, and support content.
  • Discovery is a core business function, and poor search creates friction in registration, commerce, and engagement workflows.
  • Algolia is recommended because it provides high-performance federated search, predictive autocomplete, personalization capabilities, relevance tuning, and strong mobile performance.
Identity Auth0 / Okta / AWS Cognito - default
  • Varsity.com needs centralized identity management because users currently move between multiple disconnected systems including myVarsity, Varsity TV, ecommerce, shop.varsity, My Team Shop, and brand sites.
  • Satish's update on BSN Project Passport: Project Passport continues to make strong progress toward establishing a unified identity platform across the BSN Sports ecosystem, creating a seamless and secure experience for customers while laying the foundation for future digital commerce, personalization, and customer engagement capabilities.
  • This week, both engineering pods successfully completed proof-of-concept implementations using Auth0 and AWS Cognito, demonstrating working Single Sign-On experiences between Sideline and BSN Sports.
  • While intentionally built as rapid prototypes to validate feasibility rather than production-ready solutions, the effort confirmed that both approaches can support the target architecture and migration strategy.
  • In parallel, the enterprise identity strategy white paper was distributed to the Enterprise Architecture team for review, with feedback actively being incorporated and finalization targeted for June 9.
  • These milestones represent important steps toward delivering a scalable, enterprise-wide identity and access management platform that will simplify customer access, strengthen security, and improve operational efficiency across the business.
  • Fragmented authentication creates poor user experience and limits personalization.
  • Auth0 and Okta are recommended because they support enterprise-grade SSO, secure authentication, role-based access, social login, MFA, and cross-platform identity orchestration.
Personalization Dynamic Yield / Optimizely
  • Varsity.com needs personalization because its users include athletes, parents, coaches, gym owners, fans, and school administrators with very different goals and workflows.
  • Modern users expect contextual, role-aware experiences that reduce friction and improve engagement.
  • Dynamic Yield and Optimizely are recommended because they support behavioral targeting, audience segmentation, real-time recommendations, experimentation, and scalable personalization strategies.
Analytics GA4 + Snowflake + CDP
  • Varsity.com needs enterprise analytics because the ecosystem spans registrations, streaming, commerce, marketing, and engagement across multiple platforms that currently likely operate in silos.
  • Unified analytics are required to understand customer journeys, optimize conversion, and support data-driven decisions.
  • GA4 provides behavioral web analytics, Snowflake supports scalable enterprise data warehousing, and a CDP unifies cross-platform customer profiles and attribution.
DAM Cloudinary / Bynder / MediaValet already in use
  • Varsity.com needs a digital asset management platform because the ecosystem depends heavily on photos, videos, livestream content, branding assets, event imagery, and marketing materials across many brands and channels.
  • Without centralized DAM capabilities, media operations become fragmented and inefficient.
  • Cloudinary is recommended for developer-friendly media optimization and dynamic delivery, while Bynder is recommended for stronger enterprise brand governance and workflow management.
Video / Streaming Existing Varsity TV integrations
  • Varsity.com needs deep streaming integration because livestreaming and video content are core engagement and retention drivers for the Varsity ecosystem.
  • Varsity TV already has an established audience and operational infrastructure, making integration more valuable than replacement.
  • The future platform should orchestrate Varsity TV experiences more seamlessly within the broader ecosystem rather than maintaining disconnected user journeys.
Hosting Vercel / AWS
  • Varsity.com needs modern cloud hosting because the platform experiences seasonal traffic spikes during major competitions, livestream events, and registration periods.
  • The current ecosystem requires scalability, reliability, global performance, and rapid deployment capabilities.
  • Vercel is recommended for optimized Next.js deployments and frontend velocity, while AWS provides enterprise-grade infrastructure, scalability, security, and backend extensibility.
APIs GraphQL + REST
  • Varsity.com needs an API-first integration strategy because the future platform must orchestrate multiple backend systems including registration, streaming, commerce, identity, analytics, and CMS platforms.
  • APIs are foundational to composable architecture and future scalability.
  • GraphQL is recommended for flexible frontend orchestration and reduced over-fetching, while REST remains valuable for operational integrations and third-party interoperability.
Experimentation Optimizely / LaunchDarkly
  • Varsity.com needs experimentation capabilities because modernization requires continuous UX optimization, feature testing, conversion improvements, and phased rollout strategies across a large user base.
  • Experimentation reduces risk and enables data-driven product decisions.
  • Optimizely is recommended for enterprise experimentation and personalization, while LaunchDarkly is recommended for feature flagging and controlled progressive releases.
Customer Data Customer Data Platform (CDP) / Segment?
  • Varsity.com needs a unified customer data layer because user interactions are fragmented across registrations, livestreams, commerce, content engagement, ecommerce, channels, and support systems.
  • Without unified customer intelligence, personalization and lifecycle engagement remain limited.
  • A CDP is recommended because it centralizes customer identity, behavioral data, segmentation, attribution, and cross-platform activation for marketing and personalization initiatives.
  • Segment aligns best with that direction because it is strongest as a customer data infrastructure layer, identity orchestration platform, and integration hub across distributed systems.
