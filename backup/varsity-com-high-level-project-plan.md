# Varsity.com High-Level Project Plan

Assumption: project work begins September 1, 2026. This plan assumes the future Varsity.com is a customer-friendly front door into Varsity's broader ecosystem: Events, myVarsity, Varsity TV, Shop, My Team Shop, CLI Studios, Yearbook Order Center, eShare, rep-supported catalog paths, Knowledge Hub, News, and Support.

## Project Goals

- Make Varsity.com feel simple, organized, and task-oriented even though the business is complex.
- Preserve current Varsity.com information while improving findability, navigation, and customer journeys.
- Treat Cheer and Dance as separate top-level entry points.
- Make Events a shared engine for camps, competitions, schedules, results, tickets, event gear, Watch Online, and myVarsity handoffs.
- Support Yearbook, Performing Arts, Fashion and Uniforms, Watch Online, Shop, News, Knowledge Hub, and Support without overwhelming top navigation.
- Build an SEO and AI-search-ready content foundation around helpful, authoritative answers.
- Improve handoffs to external or specialized platforms so customers know where they are going and why.

## Workstreams

### Program and Governance

- Confirm executive sponsor, product owner, project manager, and decision-making group.
- Define what must launch in Release 1 vs. what can follow.
- Establish weekly core team meetings and biweekly executive reviews.
- Maintain decision log, risk log, dependency log, and launch checklist.

### User Experience and IA

- Validate the proposed top-level IA: Cheer, Dance, Events, Yearbook, Performing Arts, Fashion and Uniforms, Watch Online, Shop.
- Confirm mobile priority navigation: Cheer, Dance, Events, myVarsity.
- Finalize page templates for home, category hubs, audience pages, event finder, event detail, Knowledge Hub, News, Support, uniform paths, directory pages, and handoff pages.
- Test key journeys with coaches, parents, athletes, fans, yearbook advisers, directors, and internal stakeholders.

### Content and SEO

- Audit existing Varsity.com content and map keep, merge, rewrite, retire, or redirect decisions.
- Build the SEO and AI-search content strategy around direct-answer pages and helpful guides.
- Prioritize Knowledge Hub topics for Cheer, Dance, Band, Yearbook, tryouts, camps, rules, rankings, and participation pathways.
- Define structured data needs: Organization, WebSite, BreadcrumbList, Event, Article, FAQ where eligible, Product, Course, VideoObject, and LocalBusiness/Place only where appropriate.
- Create metadata, URL, internal linking, and redirect standards.

### Events and Commerce Handoffs

- Define event data requirements: event name, brand, activity, audience, type, date, location, registration status, myVarsity status, tickets, event gear, schedule, results, watch status, and related news.
- Confirm rules: camps apply to School Cheer and School Dance only; camp events do not show tickets; competitions may show tickets and event gear.
- Map myVarsity handoffs for register, manage, check status, and make payment.
- Map ticketing, event gear, Varsity TV, schedule, results, and support paths.

### Fashion, Uniforms, Shop, and Sales Paths

- Confirm separate paths for School Cheer Uniforms, All Star Cheer Uniforms, School Dance Uniforms, All Star Dance Uniforms, Band Wear, and Color Guard Wear.
- Define catalog and rep-routing requirements for each path.
- Clarify how My Team Shop appears as private team commerce.
- Keep Shop positioned as individual commerce for parents, athletes, fans, and anyone buying products or event merchandise.

### Technology and Integrations

- Confirm platform/CMS approach, hosting, search, content workflow, redirects, analytics, and release process.
- Define integration strategy for event data, myVarsity, Varsity TV, Shop, My Team Shop, CLI Studios, Yearbook Order Center, eShare, and rep/contact flows.
- Decide whether the cheer gym and dance studio directory is in Release 1, pilot, or future phase.
- Define data ownership, update frequency, and operational support for event data and directory data.

### Analytics and Measurement

- Define KPIs before build starts.
- Track both on-site engagement and handoff success.
- Recommended KPIs:
  - Find an event usage and completion
  - myVarsity handoff clicks
  - Ticket and event gear handoff clicks
  - Varsity TV handoff clicks
  - Uniform catalog and rep-routing clicks
  - Shop and My Team Shop handoff clicks
  - Search terms with no results
  - Organic traffic to Knowledge Hub, Events, Cheer, Dance, Yearbook, and Performing Arts
  - Accessibility, performance, and Core Web Vitals

## Release 1 Recommended Scope

Release 1 should prioritize the highest-value customer journeys and the information architecture foundation.

- Homepage with hero modules and category content modules.
- Top-level pages: Cheer, Dance, Events, Yearbook, Performing Arts, Fashion and Uniforms, Watch Online, Shop.
- School and All Star paths for Cheer and Dance.
- Events finder with search, date filtering, event detail pages, and task handoffs.
- Event detail logic for registration/status/payment, tickets, event gear, Watch Online, schedule, results, and related event-specific content.
- Fashion and Uniforms paths for Cheer, Dance, Band Wear, and Color Guard Wear.
- Support hub and handoff explanation pages.
- News hub and contextual news modules.
- Knowledge Hub foundation with priority guides.
- Analytics, redirects, technical SEO, accessibility, and performance standards.

## Release 1.5 or Release 2 Candidates

- Full cheer gym and dance studio directory, depending on verified data source and governance.
- Personalization by role: coach, parent, athlete, fan, adviser, director.
- Saved/favorited events.
- More advanced event recommendations.
- Expanded Knowledge Hub content library.
- Deeper Varsity TV integration.
- Deeper commerce/event merchandise integration.
- Location-aware content and local event discovery.
- More robust social channel directory by brand and platform.

## Major Risks and Mitigations

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scope grows too large | Launch delay and diluted focus | Define Release 1 and future-phase backlog early |
| Event data is incomplete or inconsistent | Poor customer experience | Assign data owners and create event data standards early |
| Content migration is underestimated | SEO loss and launch delay | Start audit early and use keep/merge/rewrite/retire decisions |
| Handoff platforms are unclear | Users get lost after leaving Varsity.com | Create explicit handoff pages and ownership map |
| Directory lacks verified source data | Trust and maintenance risk | Treat directory as pilot/future phase unless governance is ready |
| SEO is added too late | Missed organic and AI-search opportunity | Build SEO into templates, content model, schema, and QA |
| Stakeholder approvals slow down | Timeline slips | Use phase gates and decision log |
| Accessibility/performance issues appear late | Launch risk | Test continuously, not just before launch |

## Launch Criteria

- Core customer journeys work on mobile and desktop.
- Navigation and search help users find tasks without understanding internal business structure.
- Event finder and event detail pages show only applicable actions.
- myVarsity, ticketing, event gear, Varsity TV, Shop, My Team Shop, CLI Studios, Yearbook Order Center, eShare, rep, and support handoffs are clear.
- Content owners approve launch pages.
- Redirect map is complete and tested.
- Metadata, schema, canonical tags, sitemap, robots rules, and analytics are validated.
- Accessibility and performance checks pass agreed standards.
- Support, customer service, and internal teams know what is changing.
- Post-launch monitoring plan is active.
