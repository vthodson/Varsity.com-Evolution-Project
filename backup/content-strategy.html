Content Strategy

Key Insights

1

Create Highly Structured Content

AI systems prefer content that is predictable, chunkable, and semantically organized.

Best Practices

  • Use clear headings, FAQ sections, tables, bullet lists, short paragraphs, explicit labels, and structured metadata.

Good

Competition Registration Deadlines

  • Registration opens: August 15
  • Registration closes: October 1
  • Payment deadline: October 10

Bad

Large narrative paragraphs with buried information.

2

Answer Questions Directly

AI systems heavily favor direct-answer formatting, especially for featured snippets, AI overviews, and retrieval systems.

Best Practice

  • Use explicit headings like "What is...", "How do I...", "When does...", and "Where can I...".

Example: "How do I register for a Varsity competition?" Then answer immediately and clearly.

3

Use Strong Entity Clarity

AI retrieval systems rely heavily on entities: brands, events, locations, products, organizations, and relationships.

Varsity should clearly reinforce UCA, NCA, NDA, Summit, Worlds, Varsity TV, myVarsity, and related entities.

Best Practices

  • Use full names consistently.
  • Avoid ambiguous acronyms without context.
  • Reinforce relationships between entities.

Example: "The UCA National High School Cheerleading Championship is hosted by Varsity Spirit."

4

Create Canonical Source Pages

AI models prioritize authoritative source pages. Varsity should create definitive pages, evergreen pages, and canonical event resources.

Examples

  • Dedicated pages for competition rules, registration dates, scoring systems, event schedules, livestream access, qualification requirements, and similar high-value information.
  • Not fragmented PDFs or scattered announcements.
5

Reduce Content Fragmentation

AI systems struggle when information exists across PDFs, duplicated pages, disconnected subdomains, or inconsistent formats.

Goal

Centralize and normalize important information.

6

Implement Structured Data (Schema Markup)

This is one of the highest-impact improvements.

Content Type Schema
Events Event
Videos VideoObject
FAQs FAQPage
Organizations Organization
Articles Article
Livestreams BroadcastEvent
Products Product
Locations Place
Breadcrumbs BreadcrumbList

Schema helps AI systems identify content types, understand relationships, and extract trustworthy answers.

7

Improve Content Freshness Signals

AI systems increasingly prioritize recent, updated, trustworthy content.

Best Practices

  • Show "last updated" dates.
  • Maintain current schedules and results.
  • Archive outdated events properly.
  • Update evergreen pages annually.
8

Optimize for Semantic Search, Not Just Keywords

Modern AI retrieval is semantic. Topic depth matters more than keyword stuffing.

Varsity Should Create

  • Comprehensive event hubs.
  • Complete guides.
  • Interconnected content.
  • Contextual relationships.

Example: A Summit page should connect registration, livestreams, schedules, rules, hotels, scoring, results, merchandise, and FAQs.

9

Improve Crawlability & Accessibility

AI systems depend heavily on accessible HTML, clean rendering, crawlable content, and page performance.

Important Technical Requirements

  • Server-side rendering (SSR).
  • Fast mobile performance.
  • Clean semantic HTML.
  • Accessible navigation.
  • Minimal hidden JavaScript-rendered content.

This is a major reason Next.js is valuable.

10

Build Authority & Trust Signals

AI systems increasingly evaluate authority, consistency, and source trustworthiness.

Varsity Should

  • Maintain consistent branding.
  • Reinforce official ownership.
  • Clearly identify official event information.
  • Avoid duplicate or conflicting pages.
  • Centralize authoritative data.
11

Create AI-Friendly Event Hubs

Varsity's biggest AI opportunity is structured event intelligence. Each major event should become a canonical knowledge hub.

Example Structure: UCA National Championship

  • Dates
  • Location
  • Registration
  • Qualification
  • Schedule
  • Livestream
  • Results
  • FAQs
  • Travel
  • Merchandise
  • Rules
