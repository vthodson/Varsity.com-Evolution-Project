Varsity.com Information Architecture V2

A front-door IA that treats Camps and Competitions as Events, gives Dance and CLI Studios a stronger path, and keeps Yearbook and Performing Arts visible while preserving current Varsity.com information.

Core Recommendation

Events is the engine Camps, competitions, special events, Spirit Days, workshops, performing arts events, tickets, event gear, status, and coverage should share one discovery model.
Dance has a real lane Dance should surface School, All Star, camps, competitions, rules, results, fashion, coverage, UDA/NDA, and CLI Studios.
Varsity.com routes tasks Many tasks finish in myVarsity, the Events Registration and Management Platform, Varsity TV, ticketing, Shop, private My Team Shop, CLI Studios, Yearbook Order Center, eShare, Stanbury, or rep/contact workflows.
Fashion and Uniforms is flat Route directly to team apparel, Cheer, Dance, Band Wear, Color Guard, catalogs, inspiration, rep help, My Team Shop, or Shop.

Recommended Primary Navigation

Primary nav Purpose
CheerCheer camps, competitions, results, schedules, rules/scoring, Fashion and Uniforms, Watch, School, All Star, Youth/Rec, and support.
DanceSchool, All Star, dance camps, competitions, results, schedules, rules/scoring, Fashion and Uniforms, Watch, UDA/NDA, CLI Studios, and support.
EventsFind camps, competitions, special events, Spirit Days, workshops, schedules, results, tickets, event gear, watch links, and register/manage/check status/make payment paths.
YearbookAdviser, student, family, school, order, eShare, login, workshop, and rep paths.
Performing ArtsBand, color guard, Stanbury, catalog, event, and rep paths.
Fashion and UniformsTeam apparel orders, coach-led uniform needs, catalogs, sizing, inspiration, rep help, and private My Team Shop access. Anyone can browse catalogs and uniform/fashion inspiration.
Watch OnlineVarsity TV live, upcoming, replay, event coverage, and Varsity Cheer League content.
ShopIndividual commerce for products and event merchandise. Anyone can shop, but the main audiences are parents, athletes, and fans.

Mobile, Footer, and Social

Surface Recommendation
Mobile top navUse an always-visible priority row: Cheer, Dance, Events, myVarsity. Keep the full primary navigation inside a left-side hamburger Menu beside the Varsity V logo. Help remains contextual, in search, footer, and support paths.
Footer: ExploreRepeat the primary IA: Cheer, Dance, Events, Yearbook, Performing Arts, Fashion and Uniforms, Watch Online, Shop.
Footer: Complete a TaskRegister/manage/status/pay, find an event, buy tickets, buy event gear, find My Team Shop, contact a rep.
Footer: SupportSupport hub, event help, myVarsity help, Fashion and Uniforms help, Yearbook help, Varsity TV help.
Footer: CompanyAbout, education and safety, careers, press, privacy, terms, and trust/corporate links.
Social channelsPlace major official platforms in the footer and link to an all-social directory organized by master brand, activity, program, event, and platform.
School / All Star brand moduleOn Cheer and Dance School/All Star pages, show appropriate brand icon shortcuts. Each shortcut opens Events filtered by activity, audience, and brand.

AI-Powered Search Readiness

Need IA response
Clear entitiesCanonical pages for Varsity, CLI Studios, Dance, events, camps, competitions, Fashion and Uniforms, My Team Shop, Yearbook, Performing Arts, products, videos, guides, and support topics.
Clear relationshipsStrong cross-links between Dance, CLI Studios, UDA/NDA, events, Fashion and Uniforms, Watch, rules, results, and support.
Direct answersConcise orientation, key facts, task CTAs, destination expectations, support route, and last-updated date.
Technical eligibilityPages should be crawlable, indexable, internally linked, textually clear, and eligible to show snippets.
Private commercePublic My Team Shop explainer/access pages can be visible, but individual school/team shop URLs should be private and noindex.
Structured dataUse Organization, WebSite, Breadcrumb, Event, Product, Course, Video, FAQ, and Article schema when it matches visible content.
